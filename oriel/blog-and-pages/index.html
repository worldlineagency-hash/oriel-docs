Blog and pages

Blog

The Blog posts page lists your posts.

  • Feature the latest post shows the newest post large at the top.
  • Show tag filter adds buttons that filter posts by tag.
  • Posts per page, Columns on desktop, Show date and Show excerpt set up the list. Posts use their excerpt when you've written one, or else their first paragraph.

Blog post

The Blog post section can show the featured image, date, author, tags, comments and more posts from the blog (Show featured image, Show date, Show author, Show tags, Show comments, Show more posts from the blog).

Comments appear when comments are turned on for the blog in Content > Blog posts > Manage blogs. When comments need approval, customers see a message saying so after they post.

Pages

Pages use the Page section. Content width sets a Narrow or Wide reading column. Headings, lists, quotes and links in your page content are styled to match the theme.

About and FAQ templates

Oriel includes two page templates with sections already in place. Choose one in the page's Theme template setting in Shopify admin, then edit the sections in the theme editor:

  • about: your page text, then a story with an image, three numbered values, a second image with text, and a link to shop.
  • faq: your page text, then two groups of questions and answers marked up for search engines, and a link to your contact page.

Keep the page's own text short, such as an introduction: the sections below it hold the rest. You can create more templates from the theme editor's template menu.

Contact page

Create a page and choose the contact template in the page's Theme template setting. The Contact form section can show the page content, and optional phone number and order number fields. Add Contact detail blocks for your email, phone or opening hours. Messages are sent to your store's email address.

Policy pages

Write your policies in Settings > Policies. Oriel shows each policy with breadcrumbs, a large title and spaced sections, beside a menu of all your policies (a row of buttons on phones). When your store has a page with the handle contact, a "Questions about our policies?" link to it appears under the menu. The footer links to your policies when Show policy links is on.

Page not found and password pages

  • The Page not found page offers search and links to your collections and home page.
  • The Password page shows while your store is password protected. It can show your logo, a heading, the message you set in Online Store > Preferences and an email signup (Show email signup).

Gift cards

When a customer buys a gift card, the gift card page shows its value, code, a QR code to scan in store, a button to copy the code, Apple Wallet and printing.
