Accessibility and performance

Accessibility

Oriel is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

  • Every control works with a keyboard and shows a visible focus outline.
  • Menus, drawers, the search window and the image viewer are announced correctly by screen readers, and return focus to where the customer was when they close.
  • Moving content can be paused, and stops for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
  • Form errors appear under each field, in your store's language.

To keep your store accessible:

  • Add alt text to product images and other meaningful images.
  • Keep enough contrast between text and background colors when you edit color schemes.
  • Use headings in order in your page content: a level 2 heading before a level 3 heading.

Performance

Oriel loads only the code each page needs, sizes images for each screen and loads images further down the page as customers scroll. To keep your store fast:

  • Upload images at least 2000 px wide, but compress them first: the file size matters more than the dimensions.
  • Remove apps you no longer use. Many apps add code to every page.
  • Keep the number of sections on your home page reasonable.
