Product pages

The Product information section is made of blocks you can reorder, add or remove. Below it, Related products shows recommendations that Shopify creates automatically.

Blocks

BlockWhat it shows
TitleThe product title. Show product type adds the type above it.
VendorThe product's vendor.
PriceThe price, with the compare-at price and percentage saved for products on sale, and the unit price where you've set one. Show tax and shipping note adds a line about taxes and shipping. Show Shop Pay Installments shows installment payments when they're available to your store.
Variant pickerOptions as buttons or dropdowns (Style). Color options show the swatches you set for option values in Shopify admin. Options that are sold out or don't exist with the current selection are crossed out.
Inventory statusIn stock, out of stock or low stock. Low stock threshold sets when "Low stock" shows.
Quantity selectorMinus and plus buttons around the quantity.
Buy buttonsThe add to cart button. Show dynamic checkout buttons adds buttons such as Shop Pay and Apple Pay. Show recipient form for gift cards lets customers send a gift card to someone else on a chosen date.
Pickup availabilityLocations where the product can be picked up, when local pickup is set up.
DescriptionThe product description.
Collapsible tabA row that opens to show text, or the content of a page, for example materials, sizing, care or shipping.
Complementary productsProducts that go well together, set up in the Shopify Search & Discovery app.
ShareShares the product with the device's share menu, or copies the link.
Text, Custom Liquid and app blocksYour own text, code or content from apps.

Purchase options and subscriptions

Products with selling plans (subscriptions, pre-orders or try before you buy) show Purchase options inside the buy buttons: one-time purchase, unless the product can only be bought with a plan, and each plan the selected variant supports, with its price and saving. The product price follows the chosen option. This works with the Shopify Subscriptions app and other apps that create selling plans; no app block is needed. The cart shows the plan name on each line.

Media

  • Layout: Thumbnails shows one large image with thumbnails; Stacked shows images one under another.
  • Image ratio: Portrait, Square or Adapt to image.
  • Open images in a full-screen viewer: customers can click an image to view the gallery full screen.
  • Autoplay videos: product videos play muted when they appear.

On phones, images become a row customers can swipe through, with a counter. Selecting a variant shows its image.

Other settings

  • Keep product information visible while scrolling: the details column stays in view next to long galleries on larger screens.
  • Show add to cart bar on mobile: a bar with the price and add to cart button appears on phones once the main button scrolls out of view.
