Quick add and cart

Quick add#

Turn on Show add to cart button in a section with product cards, such as Featured collection or Product grid.

  • Products with a single variant show Add to bag and are added straight to the cart.
  • Products with options, or with purchase options, show Choose options. It opens a panel where customers pick a variant, quantity and purchase option without leaving the page. View full details opens the product page with the chosen variant.

On touch screens the button is always shown; on other screens it appears when customers hover over or focus the card.

Cart type#

Choose Cart type in Theme settings > Cart:

  • Drawer: adding a product opens the cart in a panel at the side of the page.
  • Page: customers stay where they are and see a short message with a link to the cart page.

In the cart#

  • Customers can change quantities and remove items. Removing an item offers Undo.
  • If a customer asks for more than you have in stock, the cart shows the quantity available and explains why.
  • Each line shows its options, subscription plan, discounts and unit price. Prices before a sale or subscription saving are crossed out.
  • Show cart note adds an order note, in the cart drawer and on the cart page.
  • Automatic discounts are shown on each line and in the total. Customers enter discount codes at checkout.
  • On phones, the cart page shows a bar with the total and checkout button until the order summary scrolls into view.
