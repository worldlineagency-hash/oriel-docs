Frequently asked questions

How do I change the fonts?

In Theme settings > Typography. Choose fonts for headings, body text and accent text from Shopify's font library.

How do I add a dropdown menu?

In Content > Menus, drag a link under another link to nest it. Nested links open in a dropdown on larger screens and in the mobile menu.

Why don't filters appear on my collection?

Install the Shopify Search & Discovery app and add filters there. Check that Show filters is on in the Product grid section, and that your products have the information the filter uses, for example product types or variant options.

Why don't I see the country/region or language selector?

They appear when your store sells in more than one country or has more than one published language. Add countries in Settings > Markets and languages in Settings > Languages.

How do I offer subscriptions?

Install a subscription app, such as Shopify Subscriptions, and create selling plans for your products. Oriel shows them as purchase options on the product page without an app block. See Product pages.

Why doesn't Follow on Shop appear in the footer?

It only shows when Shop Pay is turned on for your store.

Can I add a wishlist or product reviews?

Yes, with an app from the Shopify App Store. Most apps add an app block that you can place on product pages in the theme editor.

How do I add code?

Use a Custom Liquid section or block. Duplicate your theme first. For bigger changes, consider hiring a Shopify Partner.

How do I update Oriel?

When an update is available, Shopify shows it in Online Store > Themes. Updating adds a new copy of the theme with your settings, which you can review before publishing it.
