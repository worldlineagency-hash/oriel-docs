Languages and markets

Countries and currencies#

Add countries in Settings > Markets. When you sell in more than one country, the footer shows a country/region selector (Show country/region selector in the footer section). Turn on Show currency codes in Theme settings > Currency format so customers can tell currencies with the same symbol apart.

Languages#

Oriel's storefront text is translated into English, German, French, Spanish and Turkish, and the theme editor is available in English and Turkish. Add languages in Settings > Languages. When your store has more than one published language, the footer shows a language selector (Show language selector).

To change any text in the theme, such as the button labels, go to Online Store > Themes > … > Edit default theme content.
