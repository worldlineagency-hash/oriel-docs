Product pages

The Product information section is built from blocks. Drag them to change the order, or hide one with the eye icon.

Media gallery#

Images, videos (uploaded or YouTube/Vimeo) and 3D models from the product's media. The first image is the main image on cards; a square, plain-background image works best. When a variant has its own image, choosing the variant shows it. Thumbnails show a play icon on videos; a video plays when its slide is shown and pauses when the customer moves on.

Blocks#

BlockWhat it shows
TitleThe product title, with the subtitle from the product's Subtitle field when Show subtitle is on.
RatingStars from a reviews app that fills Shopify's standard rating fields. Hidden when there are no ratings.
PricePrice, sale price, unit price and tax note, plus Shop Pay Installments when available.
Variant pickerPills or Dropdown. Show swatches uses the colors and images from Shopify's swatch settings. Sold-out combinations are marked.
Purchase optionsOne-time purchase and subscription plans from your subscription app (selling plans), with the saving shown. Products that can only be bought as a subscription start with the first plan selected.
SupplyHow long one pack lasts and the price per day, from Units per day and the number of units in the variant (or Servings per package).
Buy buttonsQuantity, add to cart and dynamic checkout buttons. For gift cards, an optional recipient form.
Pickup availabilityLocal pickup status for the selected variant, from Settings > Locations.
Complementary productsA short list of products that go well together, chosen in the block, with quick add buttons. The product itself is left out.
Collapsible rowA heading and content: your own text, the product description or a page's content.
TextA short line, for example a supplement disclaimer.
Custom Liquid and app blocksCode or app blocks, for example reviews.

Sticky add to cart#

Show sticky add to cart shows a bar with the product, variant and add to cart button once the main button scrolls out of view.

Sections below the product#

  • Related products: Shopify's automatic recommendations.
  • Complementary products section: products Shopify suggests as complements, set up in the Search & Discovery app.
  • Nutrition table, Ingredient spotlight, Image comparison, FAQ, Image with text overlay: see Product content.

Product templates#

Salvia includes three product templates: Default, featured (with nutrition table and ingredient spotlight) and powder (with a how-to-mix comparison). Choose a product's template in Shopify admin on the product page, under Theme template.
