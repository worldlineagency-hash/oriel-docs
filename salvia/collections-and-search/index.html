Collections and search

Collection pages use two sections: Collection banner and Product grid. The search page uses Search results.

Collection banner#

  • Breadcrumbs, the collection description and the collection image (set in Shopify admin on the collection).
  • Image layout: Beside the title (keeps products near the top), Wide banner, or Full width, image's own shape (nothing cropped). Set a focal point on the image in admin to choose what stays in view.
  • Collection shortcuts: a scrolling row of links to other collections.

Product grid#

  • Products per page: 12, 24, 36 and 48 fill whole rows in two, three and four columns.
  • Pagination: a Load more button or page numbers.
  • Show grid size switcher lets customers switch between large cards (three columns on desktop, one on phones) and small cards (four on desktop, two on phones).
  • Enable filtering with Horizontal or Sidebar layout, and Enable sorting.
  • Card options: second image on hover, product type and quick view.

Filters#

Salvia shows the filters you set up in Shopify's Search & Discovery app: availability, price, product type, vendor, tags, variant options (with swatches) and metafields. Filters update the products without reloading the page.

Quick view#

The plus button on product cards opens a quick view with media, variants and add to cart. Products that need a selling plan show a link to choose a plan on the product page.

Search#

Search shows products, articles and pages in tabs, with filters and sorting for products. With no results, it shows Popular searches and collections to try.
