Sections

Add sections to any page with Add section in the theme editor. Drag sections to reorder them, and use the eye icon to hide one without deleting it.

Settings every section shares#

Every section has a Color scheme and Top padding and Bottom padding (smaller on phones). Sections built from blocks show a dashed hint in the editor when all blocks are removed, and hide on the storefront when they have nothing to show.

Home page sections#

SlideshowUp to five slides with an image (and optional mobile image) or video, eyebrow, heading, text and two buttons. Tabs under the slides show each slide's Tab label. Height on desktop is a minimum: the slideshow grows if the text needs more room. Turn on Use as the page's main heading (H1) on one slide when the page has no other H1. Rotate slides automatically and Time per image slide control autoplay; a video slide moves on when its video ends. Autoplay pauses on hover and keyboard focus, and visitors can pause it. Text box position puts the card on the left or right, and Accessible name is read by screen readers.

Shop the lookA lifestyle image with numbered dots, one Product block each. Place a dot with its position settings. Customers can tick products and add them all to the cart.
Ingredient listIngredient cards with an image, source, name and text.
TestimonialsCustomer quotes, each with a name and the product it's about. Write real quotes only.
Collapsible contentA short FAQ with a heading, text and link beside it.
Blog postsThe latest articles from a blog, with date, first tag and reading time.

Routine sections#

Salvia's routine page (template page.routine) uses three sections:

  • Routine header: the page title, intro and up to four short steps.
  • Routine builder: one Moment block per time of day, each with its products. Customers tick products, see the total and add the whole routine to the cart. Products that need a selling plan link to their product page instead.
  • Featured product: one product with price and buy buttons, built from blocks.

To make a routine page: create a page in Online Store > Pages, choose the template page.routine, then add products to each moment in the theme editor.

Video#

  • Upload a Video, or paste a link in Or a YouTube / Vimeo link (used when no video is uploaded).
  • Cover image is shown until the video is played; without one, the video's own preview is used. The video and the YouTube or Vimeo player load only when the customer presses play.
  • Video description is read by screen readers: describe what the video shows.
  • Shape: the video's own shape, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1 or 9:16. The video stays within most of the screen height, so tall videos aren't cut off. Full page width shows it edge to edge, with no height limit.
  • Optional eyebrow, heading and text above the video.

Scrolling text#

  • One Text block per line, each with an optional Icon and Link.
  • Time for one loop sets the speed (higher is slower) and Moves toward the direction.
  • It pauses while the pointer is over it or a link in it has keyboard focus, and doesn't move at all for visitors who prefer reduced motion. Show pause button adds a button to stop it.

Logo list#

  • One Logo block per item. Upload a Logo image; until you do, a logo placeholder shows. To show a text badge instead of a logo, choose an Icon: the block then shows the icon and its Name. The name is also the logo's alt text.
  • Short quote or note under the logo, and an optional Link.
  • Logos are shown in gray and get their colors on hover. Upload logos with a transparent background.

Only show certifications, awards and press you really have, and link to the source where you can.

Newsletter#

An email signup with eyebrow, heading, text, Small print (for example a link to your privacy policy) and an optional image beside it. Subscribers are added to your customers with email marketing consent and the tag newsletter. The success or error message shows only under the form that was sent, even when the footer has a signup too. Button label is read by screen readers; the button shows an arrow.
