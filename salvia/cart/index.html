Cart

Choose between a cart drawer and a cart page in Theme settings > Cart.

Cart drawer and cart page#

  • Quantity buttons update the cart without reloading; quick clicks are combined into one update.
  • If there isn't enough stock, the line shows the reason and the quantity goes back to what's available.
  • Discounts, unit prices, selling plans and gift card recipients show on each line.
  • Show order note adds a note field.
  • Collection shows recommended products from a collection that aren't in the cart yet.

Free shipping progress#

Set the threshold in Theme settings > Cart and turn on Show free shipping progress in the cart sections. It's hidden for visitors who shop in another currency, because the threshold is in your store currency.

Empty cart#

When the cart is empty it shows a heading, text, a button, a menu of links (for example your collections) and an extra link, all set in the cart sections.
