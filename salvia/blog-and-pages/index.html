Blog and pages

Journal (blog)#

  • Show the newest article large, Show topics (from article tags), excerpts, dates and reading time.
  • On articles: featured image, date, reading time, author, share buttons and more articles.
  • Tag an article with product:handle (for example product:daily-foundation) to show up to three products under it. These tags are hidden from the topic list.

Pages#

The Page section shows the page title, an optional eyebrow, intro, image and the page content, optionally on a card. Salvia includes templates for About, Ingredients, FAQ, Shipping and Contact pages: choose them when you create a page.

Contact page#

The Contact form section has optional phone and order number fields and can show your store email. Add Fact blocks for response times or opening hours, and a Store location section for your address.

404 page#

A message with a search field and a menu of links.

Password page#

Shown while your store is password-protected. Add a logo (and a Logo for dark backgrounds), image, heading and an optional email signup.

Gift card page#

The page customers open from their gift card email: the balance, the code with a copy button, a QR code, an Apple Wallet button and a print button.
