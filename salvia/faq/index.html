Frequently asked questions

How do I show a nutrition table for every product?

See Product content: use a template per product, or connect the settings to metafields.

How do I offer subscriptions?

Install a subscription app, such as Shopify Subscriptions, and create selling plans. Salvia shows them in the Purchase options block without an app block.

Why don't filters appear on my collection?

Add filters in the Shopify Search & Discovery app, and check that Enable filtering is on in the Product grid section.

Why doesn't the free shipping bar show?

Set a threshold above 0 in Theme settings > Cart and turn on Show free shipping progress in the cart sections. It's hidden for visitors shopping in another currency.

Why don't I see the country/region selector?

It appears when your store sells in more than one country. Add countries in Settings > Markets.

How do I add product reviews?

Install a reviews app and add its app block to the product page. Apps that fill Shopify's standard rating fields also show stars through the Rating block.

My logo disappears on a dark header.

Add a light version under Logo for dark backgrounds in the Header (and Footer) section.

Why don't Follow on Shop and dynamic checkout buttons appear?

They need Shop Pay and the related payment methods to be turned on in your store.
