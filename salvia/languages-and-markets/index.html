Languages and markets

Selling in several countries#

Add countries and currencies in Settings > Markets. Turn on the country/region selector in the announcement bar or footer. Prices, including those calculated in the browser (routine totals, shop the look), use your store's money format for the visitor's currency.

Translating your store#

Salvia's text is in English. Add languages in Settings > Languages and translate the theme's text and your content with Shopify's Translate & Adapt app. Text you type into sections is translated there too.

Things to check per market#

  • The free shipping threshold is in your store currency and is hidden in other currencies.
  • Health claims must be allowed in each country you sell to.
