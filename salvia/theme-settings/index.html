Theme settings

Open Customize > Theme settings (the gear icon) to change settings that apply to the whole store.

Colors#

Salvia uses color schemes. Each scheme has a background, text, accent, surface (cards and panels), border and button colors. Every section has a Color scheme setting, so you can alternate light and dark bands down a page.

  • Keep enough contrast between text and background: at least 4.5:1 for body text.
  • On dark schemes the Surface color can be a slightly see-through light color; Salvia lays it over the scheme's own background.
  • For a dark header, footer or password page, add a Logo for dark backgrounds in that section. Salvia uses it automatically when the scheme is dark.

Typography#

Choose a heading font and a body font from Shopify's font library, and a Font size scale for each. Headings grow with the scale; long headings wrap and sections such as the slideshow grow taller to fit them.

Layout#

Page width sets the widest the content gets, in rem (144 = 1440 px). On narrower screens the page always fills the width with comfortable side margins.

Animations#

Reveal sections on scroll fades sections in as they come into view. It's turned off automatically for visitors who ask their device for reduced motion.

Social media#

Add links to your accounts. Icons show in the announcement bar and footer when those sections have Show social media icons turned on. Empty links are hidden.

Quick view#

The color scheme of the quick view window that opens from product cards.

Cart#

  • Cart type: Drawer opens a side panel when a product is added; Page goes straight to the cart page.
  • Free shipping threshold: the order total, in your store currency, that unlocks free shipping. The cart shows a progress bar towards it. Use the same amount as your free shipping rate in Settings > Shipping and delivery. 0 hides the bar.

Navigation#

Show back to top button adds a round button that appears after one screen of scrolling, on the Right or Left. It stays out of the way of the product page's add to cart buttons, and hides while search, the menu, the cart drawer or a popup is open.
