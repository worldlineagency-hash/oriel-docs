Header and footer

The header and footer appear on every page. Edit them in the theme editor under Header and Footer in the left sidebar.

Announcement bar#

Short messages above the header, each in its own Announcement block with optional link. Rotate announcements shows them one at a time (Change every sets the speed); visitors can pause it. Turn on the country/region selector and social media icons here too.

Header#

  • Logo image and Logo width. On phones the logo is a little smaller so the icons fit.
  • Logo for dark backgrounds: used instead of the logo when the header's color scheme is dark.
  • Sticky header keeps the header at the top while scrolling.
  • Main menu: on desktop, links with children open a dropdown; links with grandchildren open a mega menu. In a mega menu, groups that link only to products show product images. Mega menu: group shown as pills picks one group (by position) to show as rounded buttons.
  • Mega menu promo blocks add an image card to the mega menu of the link you choose.
  • Highlighted link: type the exact title of a top-level link (for example Build your routine) to show it in the accent color.
  • Account menu: links shown in the account dropdown.

If there isn't room for the whole menu, it wraps onto a second line instead of running under the logo. Below 990 px the menu moves into a slide-in drawer.

Search#

The search icon opens predictive search. Before typing, it shows Popular searches, a few Collections and the first products of Products from collection. While typing, it suggests products, collections, articles and pages.

Mobile bottom bar#

An optional bar fixed to the bottom of phone screens with shop, search, account and cart links. Add it from Add section in the footer group.

Footer#

  • Newsletter block: an email signup. Subscribers are added to your customers with email marketing consent. Show Follow on Shop adds Shop's follow button when Shop Pay is on.
  • Navigation blocks: a heading and a menu each. Hide one with the eye icon and the columns rearrange.
  • Text block: free text, for example your address or opening hours.
  • Logo, Logo for dark backgrounds, tagline, social icons, country/region selector, payment icons and policy links.

Icon band and social gallery#

Icon band shows short promises such as free shipping or vegan formulas; Hide on hides it on the home page or product pages. Social gallery shows images that link to products or to your Instagram; Hide on home page avoids repeating home page photos.

Newsletter popup#

A popup with an email signup that opens after Open after seconds. Closing it hides it for Hide after closing for days; subscribing hides it for good. Add a Discount code (create it in Discounts first) to show it after signup. It never opens on the cart or password page, or on top of the cart drawer. In the theme editor it opens while the section is selected.
