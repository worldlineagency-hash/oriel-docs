Salvia documentation

Salvia is a Shopify theme for wellness, supplement and daily-care brands that sell a focused range of products. It is built around routines: what to take in the morning, what in the evening, and what belongs together. This guide explains how to set up the theme and what each setting and section does.

Before you edit code or install apps that change theme files, duplicate your theme in Online Store > Themes > … > Duplicate so you can always go back.

Set up your store#

  1. In Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes and add Salvia from the Shopify Theme Store.
  2. Click Customize to open the theme editor.
  3. Set up your menus in Content > Menus. The header uses your Main menu and the footer's Navigation blocks use the menus you choose. Menus can have three levels: nested links open in dropdowns or a mega menu on desktop and in the mobile menu.
  4. Add your logo in the Header section, your favicon in Theme settings > Favicon, and links to your accounts in Theme settings > Social media.
  5. Pick your colors in Theme settings > Colors. Salvia comes with color schemes for light, paper, sage and ink backgrounds; every section has a Color scheme setting.
  6. Replace the placeholder images and text in each section of the home page.

What makes Salvia different#

  • Routine builder: a page where customers pick products for each moment of the day and add the whole routine to the cart at once. See Sections.
  • Nutrition table and ingredient spotlight: show what's in each serving, with reference values and authorised claims. See Product content.
  • Supply and purchase options: how long a pack lasts and its price per day, plus subscription plans from your subscription app. See Product pages.
  • Shop the look: a lifestyle image with numbered dots that customers can add to the cart together.
  • Product comparison: up to four products side by side, built from your product data and tags, with add to cart buttons. See Product pages.

What's in this guide#

  • Theme settings: colors, fonts, layout, cart and navigation.
  • Header and footer: logo, menus, mega menu, search, announcements and the footer.
  • Sections: every section you can add to your pages.
  • Product pages: blocks, media, variants, purchase options and supply.
  • Product content: nutrition tables, ingredients and FAQs for each product.
  • Collections and search: filters, sorting, quick view and search.
  • Cart: the cart drawer, cart page and free shipping progress.
  • Blog and pages: journal, pages, contact, 404, password and gift card pages.
  • Languages and markets: selling in several countries and languages.
  • Accessibility and performance, FAQ and Support.

Theme version#

You can see your theme version in Online Store > Themes, under the theme name. Include it when you contact support.
